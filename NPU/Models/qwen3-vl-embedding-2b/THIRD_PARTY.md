# Third-party references

The portable integer RGB converter independently expresses FFmpeg's unscaled YUV420 integer arithmetic using C++/NumPy. Arithmetic references are identified in `runtime/qwen_embedding/portable_rgb.py` (FFmpeg n8.1, LGPL-2.1-or-later); no FFmpeg assembly or binary is redistributed here.
Backend packages, PyAV, FFmpeg/zimg and model weights remain separately installed dependencies under their own licenses. The original repository's other model licenses and notices are retained in their moved directories.
